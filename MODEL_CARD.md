# Model Card — Customer Churn Intelligence

## Intended use

This model is designed for educational and portfolio demonstration of customer churn risk scoring and retention prioritization using the IBM Telco Customer Churn sample.

## Target

`Churn = Yes` is mapped to the positive class (`1`).

## Inputs

Customer demographic, tenure, subscribed services, contract, payment method, monthly charges, and total charges. `customerID` is excluded from modeling.

## Training design

- 80/20 stratified train/test split.
- 5-fold stratified cross-validation on training data.
- Candidate models: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost.
- Selection metric: mean cross-validation ROC-AUC.
- Operating threshold: chosen from out-of-fold training probabilities using F2 score.
- Final evaluation: held-out test set.

## Evaluation metrics

Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1, F2, ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, and confusion matrix.

Exact values are generated by `python -m src.train` and saved to `models/model_metrics.json`.

## Explainability

The app attempts local SHAP explanations for individual predictions. Global estimator feature importance or coefficient magnitude is also exposed in the model-insights view.

## Risk tiers

- Low: probability < 0.30
- Medium: 0.30–0.49
- High: 0.50–0.69
- Critical: >= 0.70

Risk tiers are communication bands, separate from the model's tuned binary decision threshold.

## Business prioritization

`RevenueAtRisk = ChurnProbability × MonthlyCharges × 12`

This is a modeled expected-value heuristic and is not a realized loss estimate.

## Known limitations

- Static sample with no temporal validation.
- No causal estimate of whether a retention offer will prevent churn.
- No intervention cost, margin, or treatment capacity constraint.
- Risk tiers are heuristic bands rather than policy thresholds optimized from an operational cost matrix.
- Results should not be generalized beyond the demonstration dataset without retraining and validation.
